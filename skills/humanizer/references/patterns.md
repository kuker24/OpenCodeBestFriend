# AI Pattern Catalog

Detailed catalog of writing patterns to identify and eliminate, grouped by the five core families.

## 1. Staging (Throat-Clearing and Setup)

- **Theatrical intros:** "In today's digital landscape", "In an era of rapid technological advancement", "When it comes to...".
  - *Fix:* Cut the intro completely. Start directly with the thesis or action.
- **Announcing the topic:** "This article explores...", "We will examine the key aspects of...", "Let's dive into...".
  - *Fix:* State the findings or steps directly without announcing the writing itself.
- **Rhetorical staging:** "Have you ever wondered how...", "Imagine a world where...".
  - *Fix:* Remove hypothetical hooks in technical and professional prose.
- **Artificial scaffolding:** "First and foremost", "It is worth noting that", "Needless to say".
  - *Fix:* Delete the filler words. If it's needless to say, don't say it.

## 2. Rhythm (Cadence and Sentence Variation)

- **Uniform sentence length:** A continuous series of sentences each having 14 to 20 words.
  - *Fix:* Break some into short 4-word anchors. Expand others with natural connective clauses.
- **Predictable symmetry:** Sentences balanced with neat parallel clauses joined by "while" or "and".
  - *Fix:* Break symmetry. Vary subject placement and start sentences with prepositional phrases or verbs when natural.
- **Monotone paragraph structure:** Paragraphs strictly composed of 1 topic sentence + 2 supporting sentences + 1 concluding sentence.
  - *Fix:* Mix 1-sentence punchy paragraphs with deeper descriptive blocks.

## 3. Inflation (Puffery and Vocabulary)

- **AI vocabulary words:**
  - *Verbs:* delve, enhance, foster, garner, showcase, underscore, illuminate, demystify, embrace.
  - *Nouns:* landscape, tapestry, testament, interplay, paradigm, beacon, cornerstone, synergy.
  - *Adjectives:* pivotal, intricate, vibrant, indelible, profound, seamless, multifaceted, crucial.
  - *Fix:* Replace with everyday equivalents (e.g. "look into", "improve", "support", "collect", "show", "highlight", "clear up").
- **Breathless superlatives:** "Game-changing", "groundbreaking", "breathtaking", "revolutionize", "unmatched".
  - *Fix:* State what the tool or concept actually achieves with concrete numbers or mechanisms.
- **Empty attribution:** "Experts agree", "Industry analysts suggest", "Studies show".
  - *Fix:* Name the exact person, company, or paper, or cut the attribution.
- **False ranges:** "From startups to enterprises", "From beginners to seasoned veterans".
  - *Fix:* State the specific audience directly or omit the generic bracket.

## 4. Formatting (Structural Tells)

- **Bullet point obsession:** Converting narrative explanations or short sequences into bullet lists.
  - *Fix:* Write cohesive prose paragraphs unless the items are truly an unstructured inventory.
- **Bold lead-in lists:** Mechanical bold label followed by a colon restating the heading:
  - *Example:* "**Efficiency:** Increased efficiency across the board."
  - *Fix:* Turn into natural prose sentences.
- **Em dash epidemic (—):** Reaching for em dashes to insert parenthetical thoughts in every paragraph.
  - *Fix:* Use commas, parentheses sparingly, or split into two separate sentences.
- **Title case subheadings:** Capitalizing Every Word In Every Subheading.
  - *Fix:* Use sentence case headings ("Getting started with SQLite").
- **Emoji decorations:** Placing rocket, sparkle, or fire emojis on headings or bullet lists.
  - *Fix:* Strip decorative emojis.

## 5. Leftovers (Chatbot Artifacts and Wrap-Ups)

- **Chatbot pleasantries:** "I hope this guide was helpful!", "Feel free to reach out if you have any questions!", "Certainly, here is the...".
  - *Fix:* Delete entirely.
- **Formulaic conclusions:** "In conclusion", "To summarize", "Looking ahead, the future of X is bright".
  - *Fix:* End with the final operational point, next logical action, or leave the conclusion off.
- **Hedge stacking:** "It could potentially be argued that it might be advisable to consider...".
  - *Fix:* "Consider..." or "We recommend...".
- **Sycophancy:** "Great question! That's a fantastic observation."
  - *Fix:* Answer directly with zero conversational stroking.
