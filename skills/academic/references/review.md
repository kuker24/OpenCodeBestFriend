# Mode: Academic Review

Criterion-bound peer review and adversarial scholarly critique.

## Objective

Deliver constructive, rigorous, and unsycophantic peer review evaluation of an academic draft, proposal, or thesis chapter.

## Evaluation Axes

1. **Significance & Novelty:**
   - Does the work solve a real, non-trivial problem?
   - How clear is the delta between this work and prior state-of-the-art?

2. **Soundness & Methodology:**
   - Are mathematical formulations correct and notations consistent?
   - Is experimental design appropriate (baselines, datasets, sample sizes, controls)?
   - Are metrics well-chosen and statistically justified (confidence intervals, significance tests)?

3. **Evidence vs. Claims:**
   - Do the empirical results actually substantiate the claims made in the abstract and conclusion?
   - Is there scope creep or overclaiming (e.g. claiming "universal efficiency" based on a single benchmark)?

4. **Reproducibility & Clarity:**
   - Could an independent researcher reproduce the method with the details provided?
   - Are hyperparameters, environments, hardware, and dataset splits fully disclosed?
   - Are figures, tables, and algorithms self-contained and clear?

## Anti-Sycophancy Review Template

Structure the review without conversational praise:

- **Summary of the Paper:** Neutral, concise summary of the core thesis, method, and primary results.
- **Key Strengths:** 2-3 genuine, technically grounded contributions.
- **Major Weaknesses:** Blocking methodological flaws, missing baselines, overclaims, or unaddressed threats to validity.
- **Minor Issues:** Typos, notation ambiguities, missing references, formatting discrepancies.
- **Specific Questions for the Authors:** Concrete technical questions to resolve in revision.
