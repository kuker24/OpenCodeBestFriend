# Mode: Academic Revise

Systematic response to peer critique and manuscript revision.

## Objective

Apply peer-review or advisor feedback using a transparent, auditable revision ledger and rebuttal matrix.

## Procedure

1. **Deconstruct Feedback:**
   - Parse review comments into discrete, numbered items.
   - Categorize each: `Methodology`, `Experimentation`, `Presentation`, `Citations`, `Scope`.

2. **Formulate Response Strategy:**
   - For each item, decide the action:
     - `AGREE_AND_UPDATE`: Make the concrete textual, analytical, or experimental change.
     - `CLARIFY_IN_TEXT`: The reviewer misunderstanding indicates ambiguous wording in the text; rewrite the passage for clarity.
     - `RESPECTFULLY_REBUT`: The critique contradicts established domain assumptions or scope constraints; formulate a polite, evidence-backed rebuttal.

3. **Rebuttal Matrix Format:**
   Maintain a two-column response table:
   - **Reviewer Comment:** Exact quote or precise summary of the critique.
   - **Author Action & Text Delta:** Exact explanation of what changed and reference to the updated section (e.g. "Updated Section 4.2 to clarify the ablation baseline; added Figure 5").

4. **Diff Verification:**
   - Verify that all claims modified in the revision remain anchored to verified citations.
   - Confirm that cross-references, figure numbers, and bibliography keys remain intact.
