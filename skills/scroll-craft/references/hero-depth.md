# Hero depth (optional)

Hero depth is not required. Skip it on dashboards, Live Surface, and any brief
that asks for a static or simple open. Do not invent a marketing hero.

When used, plan independent planes before assets:

- Background, atmosphere, subject, foreground, type.
- Visible depth: different translation/scale rates, occlusion, contact anchors
  that stay planted (product on plinth, feet on ground).
- Headline readable at rest. Depth must not hide the claim.

## Motion

One camera idea. Sticky + transforms can be enough; no WebGL requirement.
Essential copy stays semantic HTML. Pause offscreen work. Reduced motion keeps
a complete still composition — no extra empty pin, no hidden content.

Pointer response is optional and never the only path. Do not lock the cursor.

## Assets

Prefer user/project images with a clear license (tier 2). Rebuild the hole
behind a cutout. Inspect alpha; a checkerboard is not transparency. Load planes
together from a complete poster fallback.

Do not ship upstream brand/media from scroll-craft. Those assets are not
cleared for redistribution.

## Mobile

Art-direct separately: crop, subject position, type size, layer order, pin
length. Type may sit above the subject on a phone even if it sits behind on
desktop. No horizontal overflow.

## Check

Opening, mid, and exit of the hero; mobile; motion-off. Distinct rates, no
duplicate subjects or holes, CTA still works. A unit test is not visual proof.
