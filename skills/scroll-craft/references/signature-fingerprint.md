# Signature and fingerprint

## Signature

One bespoke interaction that exists for this product. Coded in the page
(`data-sc-*` of your own naming or JS reading `--sc-p`). The engine stays
untouched.

**Counts:** a persistent evidence map drawn as the visitor scrolls; an SVG that
literally draws the object from `--sc-p`; a running ledger of real figures; one
control that regrades the whole page.

**Does not count:** retinted spotlight, different `tilt` degrees, extra rail
cards, another `scrub`, a kit device with a new class name.

Fallback: static/keyboard/reduced-motion equivalent. Information is not trapped
in the motion.

## Fingerprint (six dimensions)

| # | Dimension | Records |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grammar | Which grammar, or a named new one |
| 2 | Nav treatment | What chrome is for |
| 3 | Hero / opening | What the first screen does |
| 4 | Sequence of acts | Order, count, pacing |
| 5 | Close | Last screen and where the CTA lives |
| 6 | Signature | The bespoke move, in a phrase |

Compare against **available** prior builds for this project (`.scratch/scroll-craft/`
or an existing fingerprint file). Show structural differences, not only colour
and copy. Name similarities and why a structure is kept.

There is **no** hard gate of “differ on 4 of 6 against all history.” That can
force a worse design or block iteration on the same product.

No history: write `NO_HISTORY` and continue. Do not invent rows or rewrite old
ones to pass.

After shipping, append one honest row under `.scratch/scroll-craft/<slug>/`.
Do not claim global uniqueness.
