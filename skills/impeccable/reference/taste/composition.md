# Taste: Composition & Layout Diversity

Deep reference module for Impeccable.
Adapted from [taste-skill](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill) (MIT, Leonxlnx).

## 1. Layout Diversity & Anti-Repetition

- **Layout Family Budget**: On a full landing page or portfolio, vary section layouts. Never repeat the identical 3-column card grid or 50/50 split across 3 consecutive sections.
- **Zigzag Alternation Cap**: Limit alternating "image-left/text-right" then "text-left/image-right" rows to at most 2 sections in sequence. Break the rhythm with full-width statements, bento grids, or vertical cards.
- **Bento Grid Rhythms**:
  - Size bento cells exactly to the available content (3 items = 3 cells in asymmetric trio; 5 items = 5 cells). Never leave awkward blank slots.
  - Vary visual weight: combine rich media, metric callouts, and interactive previews rather than uniform plain cards.

## 2. Hero Composition

- **Viewport Fit**: The hero headline, value proposition, and primary action must be visible without scrolling at standard desktop viewport (1080p).
- **Subtext Brevity**: Keep hero subtext focused (under 25 words). If the value proposition cannot be stated in 2–3 sentences, clarify the message rather than enlarging the hero paragraph.
- **Top Padding Cap**: Cap hero top padding (`pt-20` to `pt-24`) so content sits naturally in the upper viewport.
- **Logo Wall Placement**: Place customer/trust logo strips in a dedicated section beneath the primary hero rather than cluttering the hero text stack.

## 3. Content Density & Long Lists

- Avoid monotonous 20-row bordered tables on marketing surfaces. Present top highlights prominently with an expandable disclosure or secondary link for exhaustive lists.
- For specification lists: group into logical clusters with subtle section dividers rather than a heavy hairline under every row.
- Quotes & Testimonials: Limit quote bodies to 2–3 concise sentences that land immediately.
